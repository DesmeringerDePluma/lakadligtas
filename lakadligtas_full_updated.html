How it works

1. Take a photo from a safe public place and pin the spot.

2. Rate the walkway: Passable Difficult or Blocked.

3. The student team verifies it and, with permission, shares it with the barangay contact.

4. After seven days we check again: still there, improved, or resolved.

Our goal

Make it easier to spot and address walkway obstructions, especially those that affect older adults, children, and people with disabilities, and to measure whether things actually improve.

Pilot notice: New reports are reviewed by the student team before they appear publicly. This pilot is not yet connected to a barangay office.

What happens next?

Your report receives a reference number. Save it: you can use it on the Track page. Its status moves through Pending Verified Reported to Barangay Resolved.

New reports stay private until the student team verifies them. Then they appear on the Reports list and the map.

Seven days after verification, the report is due for a follow-up check so we can see whether anything changed.

Please do not use this site for emergencies. Contact the appropriate local emergency service for immediate danger.
