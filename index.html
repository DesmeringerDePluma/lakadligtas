How it works

1. Take a photo from a safe public place and pin the spot.

2. Rate the walkway: Passable Difficult or Blocked.

3. The student team verifies it and, with permission, shares it with the barangay contact.

4. After seven days we check again: still there, improved, or resolved.

Our goal

Make it easier to spot and address walkway obstructions, especially those that affect older adults, children, and people with disabilities, and to measure whether things actually improve.

Pilot notice: This demo is not yet connected to a barangay office. Reports are stored only in this browser on this device.

What happens next?

Your report receives a reference number. Its status moves through Pending Verified Reported to Barangay Resolved.

Seven days later, it is due for a follow-up check so we can see whether anything changed.

Use Track any time to look up a report by its reference number.

Please do not use this demo for emergencies. Contact the appropriate local emergency service for immediate danger.
